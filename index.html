About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Epidemiology and Health Statistics at Xi'an Jiaotong University. From 2023 to 2025, I was a visiting Ph.D. student at Monash University in Melbourne, Australia.

My research interests lie at the intersection of Infectious Disease Modelling (HIV, STIs, etc.), Health Economics, and Clinical Trials. My Ph.D. project uses mathematical models and statistical methods to evaluate the impact and cost-effectiveness of interventions against bacterial sexually transmitted infections (STIs), such as syphilis, gonorrhoea, and chlamydia, among gay, bisexual, and other men who have sex with men. A current focus is doxycycline post-exposure prophylaxis (doxy-PEP) and its implications for antimicrobial resistance in Neisseria gonorrhoeae.
